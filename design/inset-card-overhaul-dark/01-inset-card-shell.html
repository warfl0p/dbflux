DBFlux inset card overhaul

Proposal for review before any code changes. The sidebar and status bar sit on the window ground (#191A1B). The open document is one rounded card (#121314) inset by 6 px. Labels use sentence case. Upstream's chamfered mark, the desk gaps and the in-tree failure block are gone.

Main window: editor, results and a failed connection

The failed connection gets a red dot and a short "Failed" label on its own row. Hovering the row, or pressing → on it, opens the card with the error and its actions. No rows are added to the tree.

  • Ground and card. Sidebar, rail and status bar share #191A1B. The document card is #121314 with an 8 px radius and a faint 1 px edge. This replaces the 1 px #2A2B2C desk gaps.
  • Sentence case. "Connections", "Staging", column headers and every section label lose the uppercase and the tracking.
  • Rows. 26 px tree rows with a 6 px radius. Hover and selection are neutral washes inset from the sidebar edge, as in Linear.
  • Connection state. A dot at the row's right end: green when connected, a hollow ring when idle, red when failed, with a spinner while connecting. Only a failed row adds a word.
  • Results. The result bar shows the outcome as plain text, with a segmented Grid / JSON / Chart switch and ghost actions.
